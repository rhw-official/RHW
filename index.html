Rohingya Humanitarian Workers (RHW) is dedicated to supporting, empowering, and strengthening Rohingya communities through youth engagement, protection, and education.

Learn About RHW Support Our Work

Mission & Vision

Restoring faith, empowering our people, and preparing the next generation.

Our Mission

To serve vulnerable communities with compassion, dignity, and responsibility while empowering Rohingya youth and community members to participate meaningfully in humanitarian and social development initiatives.

Our Vision

A future where Rohingya communities are educated, empowered, protected, resilient, and able to build a peaceful and dignified future, where youth and women become leaders of change.

Our Key Objectives

Promote humanitarian values, dignity, and community solidarity.

Empower Rohingya youth through leadership and volunteerism.

Support education, learning, and awareness opportunities.

Promote child protection and the safety and well-being of children.

Raise awareness on important humanitarian and social issues.

Strengthen skills and confidence of young humanitarian workers.

Our Areas of Work

Key domains where RHW makes an impact.

Youth Empowerment

Developing young people's leadership, communication, teamwork, creativity, and volunteerism.

Education & Learning

Encouraging educational opportunities, knowledge-sharing, awareness, and lifelong learning.

Child Protection

Promoting awareness and community action to help create safer and more supportive environments for children.

Community Awareness

Organizing awareness activities on humanitarian, social, educational, and community issues.

Humanitarian Support

Encouraging community-based responses and support for people facing vulnerability and hardship.

Leadership & Volunteerism

Building a culture where young people can contribute their time, skills, and ideas to meaningful community service.

Who We Are

Rohingya Humanitarian Worker (RHW) is an initiative committed to supporting marginalized communities through humanitarian efforts, education, healthcare, and advocacy.

Mission: To provide sustainable humanitarian support and educational opportunities.
Vision: A community empowered with self-reliance, dignity, and rights.

Our Key Location

Rohingya Refugee Camps, Ukhiya, Cox's Bazar, Bangladesh

Operating actively in field initiatives to ensure real impact on the ground.
